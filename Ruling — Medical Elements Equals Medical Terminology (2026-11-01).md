# Ruling — Medical Elements Equals Medical Terminology (2026-11-01)

**Provenance:** Douglas's ruling, Nov 1, 2026, ~1:41 PM CST, filed verbatim below. This ruling answers Part IV, question 2 of the Medical Elements book outline filed the same hour. It does not overwrite that outline; it sits beside it, old-with-new.
**Shiro:** PENDING.

## His ruling, verbatim

> "medical elements definitely equals medical terminology. And I give that the American definition as far as the 21st century US New World English version and that's for the tribal government first and for the United States government second."

## What the ruling settles

1. **Medical Elements = Medical Terminology.** The book's "elements" are terms — the words medicine uses — not chemical elements, and not only his loose units (virus, fungus, sputum, etc.). Those units remain the book's *content*, but the book's *elements* are defined as terminology: each element is a term, defined in American English, with its Latin second where Latin applies (per his standing naming doctrine: American first, Latin second).
2. **The definition is American, 21st-century, US New World English.** Terms are defined as they are used in present-day American English, not in classical Latin, not in British usage, and not in older American usage where usage has changed.
3. **Priority order — tribal government first, United States government second.** The book's terminology is given, in his words, for the tribal government first and for the United States government second. Filed as his priority order / dedication order. This is his direction for the book, not a claim about any government's official adoption of the book.
4. **What it does not settle:** whether any specific term's American definition differs from its Latin or standard definition — that is per-term work for each chapter, with GAPs left where a term's American definition is his coinage awaiting confirmation (e.g., "medical element" itself, "thoughtology," "mucoids," "chemiology").

## Consequence for the full book build (in progress at time of ruling)

The book builder was instructed, at 1:41 PM CST, to incorporate this ruling into the complete book: front matter and Chapter 1 must state Medical Elements = Medical Terminology, American 21st-century US New World English definition, tribal government first, United States government second, and every element table must give the American term first, Latin second where applicable.
